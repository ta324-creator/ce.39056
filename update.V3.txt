Don't think of it as a directory.

Think of it as the daily homepage of Ensenada.

When someone wakes up, they might ask:

Where should I eat today?
Are there any events tonight?
Is there traffic?
Is anyone hiring?
What's happening in the city?
Where can I find a plumber?
What's new this week?

If ConectandoEnsenada answers those questions, it becomes part of people's routine instead of being something they visit once and forget.

If it were my product strategy

I'd define the platform like this:

Discover → Everything worth exploring in Ensenada.
Connect → Jobs, businesses, services, events, and community.
Stay Informed → News and updates that make people return every day.

That combination is powerful because it serves three different user intents: exploration, action, and habit. Exploration attracts visitors, action helps them accomplish something, and habit gives them a reason to come back. That's a much stronger foundation for a long-term platform than simply being "a business directory."
