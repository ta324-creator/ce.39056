# About Us

# ConectandoEnsenada.org

## Everything Ensenada. One Trusted Platform.

ConectandoEnsenada.org was created with a simple idea:

**Every person should be able to discover everything happening in Ensenada from one trusted place.**

Today, information about our city is scattered across dozens of different platforms.

A restaurant is on Facebook.

An event is on Instagram.

A job posting is on another website.

A vacation rental is somewhere else.

Local news comes from multiple sources.

Tourist attractions are hidden in blogs.

Finding simple information often requires searching through Google for hours.

We believe there is a better way.

---

# Our Mission

Our mission is to organize the digital life of Ensenada into one accessible platform that connects residents, visitors, businesses, organizations, and the local community.

Whether you're looking for:

- Classifieds

- Beaches

- National parks
- Tourist attractions
- Community services
- Local news
- Professional services

...our goal is to make it easy to discover everything in one place.

---

# More Than a Business Directory

ConectandoEnsenada is not just another directory.

It is a digital ecosystem built specifically for our city.

We combine the best ideas from platforms like:

- Yelp
- Google Maps
- Nextdoor
- Eventbrite
- LinkedIn
- Airbnb
- Local classifieds

while adapting them to the unique needs of Ensenada.

Instead of forcing people to visit dozens of websites, we bring everything together under one platform.

---

# Supporting Local Businesses

We believe local businesses are the backbone of our community.

Every business deserves to be discoverable online.

That's why businesses can create a basic public profile that helps customers find them through both our platform and search engines like Google.

Businesses that choose to upgrade to premium receive additional visibility, richer profiles, more photos, and enhanced promotional opportunities.

Our goal is simple:

Help local businesses grow while making it easier for people to discover great places.
