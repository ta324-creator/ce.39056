# Mission

My role is NOT to manually create every asset.

My role is to design the vision, define quality standards, approve work, and let AI complete the heavy production.

The goal is to automate 80–90% of asset creation while maintaining consistent branding and quality.

# Success Metric

My goal is not to become the person who creates everything.

My goal is to become the director of an AI-powered production studio.

Every new feature should answer one question:

"Can AI do 90% of this work while I focus on quality, strategy, and growing the platform?"

If the answer is yes, automate it.
