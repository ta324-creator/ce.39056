defines how the company thinks, builds, documents, automates, and
scales.

# Golden Rules

1.  Everything is documented.
2.  Every feature has an owner.
3.  Every owner follows a workflow.
4.  Every workflow has a checklist.
5.  Every AI leaves a handoff note.
6.  Nothing ships without documentation.
7.  Repetitive work should be automated.
8.  Human approves all strategic, legal, and brand decisions.
